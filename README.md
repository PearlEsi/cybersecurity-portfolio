## How I Approach Security Work

1. **Understand the environment:** establish the system, business purpose, users, data and relevant controls.
2. **Collect evidence:** use logs, test results, configuration data and repeatable observations.
3. **Assess risk:** consider likelihood, impact, exposure and business context.
4. **Recommend proportionate action:** prioritise practical remediation and explain why it matters.
5. **Verify and document:** retest where possible, record outcomes and communicate residual risk.
